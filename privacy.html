Module Builder

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Module Builder is a free study tool for the SAT and PSAT, made by Luka Gruev. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Module Builder server, no account with us, and no advertising or analytics.

What stays on your device

The question bank PDFs and question sets you add are read in your browser. Your questions, practice modules and results are saved in your browser's storage on your device. We never receive them.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you press Sign in with Google, Module Builder asks Google for permission to use one thing: a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It uses that folder only to save a copy of your questions, modules and results, so you can pick up your progress on another computer.

  • Module Builder can only see the files it created in that folder. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • It also reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which account you're signed in with.
  • The data goes directly between your browser and Google. It is never sent to Module Builder or anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising.
  • The sign-in only lasts about an hour, and it is kept in your browser tab, not stored anywhere else.

Module Builder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • To delete the copy in Google Drive, go to Question bank and press Remove from Google Drive. You can also remove it in Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.
  • To stop Module Builder's access, press Sign out, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To delete what's saved on your device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Other services the site uses

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts and its PDF reader from cdnjs. Like any website, those services receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. Their own privacy policies apply. Links to the Desmos calculator and the College Board question bank open those sites, which have their own policies.

Children

Module Builder is meant for students preparing for the SAT and PSAT. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
